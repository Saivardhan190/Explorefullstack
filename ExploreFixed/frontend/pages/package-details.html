Package Overview

Experience the magic of Bali with our exclusive Paradise Package. This 7-day adventure takes you through the island's most breathtaking landscapes, from pristine beaches to lush rice terraces and ancient temples. Immerse yourself in the rich Balinese culture, indulge in delicious local cuisine, and unwind in luxurious accommodations.

Highlights

  • Explore the sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary in Ubud
  • Visit the iconic Tanah Lot Temple at sunset
  • Relax on the pristine beaches of Nusa Dua
  • Experience traditional Balinese dance performances
  • Trek through the stunning Tegalalang Rice Terraces
  • Enjoy a private dinner on Jimbaran Beach

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Bali

Arrive at Ngurah Rai International Airport where you'll be greeted by your guide. Transfer to your hotel in Seminyak for check-in and welcome dinner.

Day 2: Seminyak & Kuta

Explore the trendy areas of Seminyak and Kuta. Enjoy shopping, beach time, and an optional surfing lesson. Evening at leisure.

Day 3: Uluwatu & Jimbaran

Visit the clifftop Uluwatu Temple and watch the famous Kecak dance performance. Enjoy a seafood dinner on Jimbaran Beach at sunset.

Day 4: Ubud Cultural Day

Transfer to Ubud. Visit the Sacred Monkey Forest, Ubud Palace, and local art galleries. Attend a traditional Balinese cooking class.

Day 5: Tegalalang & Temples

Explore the stunning Tegalalang Rice Terraces. Visit Tirta Empul Temple and participate in a purification ceremony. Afternoon at leisure in Ubud.

Day 6: East Bali Adventure

Full-day excursion to East Bali. Visit Besakih Temple, Tirta Gangga Water Palace, and Amed Beach. Return to Ubud for farewell dinner.

Day 7: Departure

Free morning for last-minute shopping or relaxation. Transfer to the airport for your departure flight.
